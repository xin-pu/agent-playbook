# MCP Governance Working Group

Welcome to the **MCP Governance Working Group** — a collaborative community focused on refining, documenting, and stewarding [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) governance processes and community values.

## Mission

The Governance Working Group exists to refine, document, and steward MCP's governance processes and community values, providing a single home for revising the project's governance documentation as the project grows.

It also serves as an advisory voice channel — surfacing community concerns to Core Maintainers and stewarding the contribution culture that helps people advance through the [contributor ladder](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/blob/main/docs/community/contributor-ladder.mdx). Over time the WG aspires to evolve into a standing consultation forum for contested governance decisions and, longer-term, to support representative community participation in the broader MCP organization.

The full charter lives in the specification repository: [Governance Working Group Charter](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/blob/main/docs/community/working-groups/governance.mdx).

## Scope

### In Scope

- **Governance documentation stewardship** — content under `docs/community/` covering governance, contributor ladder, SEP guidelines, working and interest groups, communication, antitrust, and project policies
- **Decision recording** — defining what counts as a project decision, who ratifies it, and how it is recorded
- **Code of Conduct stewardship** — drafting, revising, and maintaining the Code of Conduct text and adjacent reporting/escalation documentation
- **Community values** — stewarding MCP's stated community values and landing the values workshop as a public artefact
- **Contribution culture and mentorship** — encouraging mentorship as a culture and stewarding movement across contributor ladder tiers
- **Voice channel** — surfacing community concerns to Core Maintainers in an advisory, non-binding capacity
- **Cross-cutting coordination** — coordinating with other WGs and IGs on governance processes that affect them

### Out of Scope

- **Specification authorship** — protocol changes go through the [SEP process](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/blob/main/docs/community/sep-guidelines.mdx) and the relevant spec-producing Working Group
- **Maintainer selection and succession** — the WG may recommend process improvements but does not pick people
- **Individual Code of Conduct moderation decisions** — incident handling lives elsewhere; the WG seeks aggregated, anonymised trend reports
- **Per-SDK governance** — owned by the [SDK Working Group](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/blob/main/docs/community/working-groups/sdk.mdx)
- **Per-WG/IG internal operations** — the WG sets framework only
- **Maintainer disputes and interpersonal conflict resolution outside the Code of Conduct** — out of scope for v0.1

## Communication

- **Primary Channel:** [MCP Discord](https://discord.com/invite/6CSzBmMkjX) → `#governance-wg`
- **Meetings:** Monthly working session, 60 minutes
  - Agendas published in `#governance-wg` at least 48 hours ahead when items are known
- **Meeting Notes:** GitHub Discussions → `Meeting Notes - Governance WG`
- **Work Tracking:** GitHub Issues and Discussions in this repository
- **Reporting:** The Lead carries concerns needing Core Maintainer attention to the monthly Core Maintainer plus WG/IG leads meeting

## Meetings

**Cadence:** Monthly
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Format:** Virtual

> **Note:** See [meet.modelcontextprotocol.io](https://meet.modelcontextprotocol.io) for meeting links and calendar.

The monthly working session is a standing slot and is not cancelled for lack of an agenda. The WG does not run office hours; open Q&A happens in `#governance-wg` and at project-wide Core Maintainer office hours.

All meetings include:
- Published agenda (48h prior, when items are known)
- Open attendance for all interested participants
