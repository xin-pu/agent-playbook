> Published Oct 26, 2026 — "Today, Anthropic, PBC filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission relating to its proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock. Anthropic intends to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol 'ANT.' The number of shares of Class A common stock to be offered and the price range for the proposed offering have not yet been determined."
